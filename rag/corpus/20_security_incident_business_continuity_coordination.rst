Security Incident Business Continuity Coordination
==================================================

Document Metadata
-----------------
- Owner: Business Continuity and Security Operations
- Approver: COO and CISO
- Review cadence: Semiannual

Purpose
-------
This playbook defines coordination between security incident response
and business continuity management during disruptive cyber events.

When to Activate
----------------
- Security incident causes prolonged service outage
- Critical business process cannot meet recovery objectives
- Cross-functional resource contention threatens restoration timelines

Coordination Model
------------------
- Incident Commander manages threat containment and eradication.
- Business Continuity Lead manages process continuity and workarounds.
- Joint command cadence aligns technical and operational decisions.

Priority Framework
------------------
- Tier 1: Life safety, legal obligations, core revenue operations
- Tier 2: Critical customer commitments and strategic internal services
- Tier 3: Non-critical supporting services

Operational Steps
-----------------
1. Confirm affected services and map to business processes.
2. Compare projected downtime to RTO/RPO thresholds.
3. Activate approved continuity workarounds.
4. Allocate constrained resources by tier priority.
5. Reassess every update interval for escalation or de-escalation.

Decision Criteria
-----------------
- Continue containment-first posture when adversary presence remains active.
- Shift to controlled restoration when threat suppression is stable.
- Invoke external recovery providers when internal capacity is insufficient.

Communication Requirements
--------------------------
- Unified status brief for executives and business owners.
- Explicit statement of service availability and workaround scope.
- Forward-looking recovery milestones with confidence levels.

Post-Restoration Activities
---------------------------
- Validate data integrity and control baseline before full cutover.
- Retire temporary workarounds with documented rollback checks.
- Measure continuity performance against RTO/RPO commitments.

Closure Criteria
----------------
- Critical processes restored within accepted thresholds
- Security and continuity risks reduced to approved residual level
- Cross-functional lessons captured and actioned
- Recovery performance metrics reported to leadership
