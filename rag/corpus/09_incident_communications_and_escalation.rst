Incident Communications and Escalation
======================================

Document Metadata
-----------------
- Owner: Incident Management Office
- Approver: CISO
- Review cadence: Quarterly

Purpose
-------
This document defines communication channels, escalation paths,
and stakeholder expectations during security incidents.

Communication Principles
------------------------
- Accuracy over speculation
- Timeliness with clear next update time
- Audience-specific language and impact framing
- Single source of truth for incident status

Severity-Based Cadence
----------------------
- Severity 1: stakeholder updates every 30 minutes
- Severity 2: updates every 60 minutes
- Severity 3: updates every 4 hours
- Severity 4: daily or closure summary only

Required Stakeholders
---------------------
- Incident Commander
- Security Operations Lead
- IT Operations Lead
- Legal and Privacy representatives (as needed)
- Business service owner
- Executive sponsor for Severity 1 and major Severity 2

Escalation Conditions
---------------------
- Material customer impact
- Regulatory reporting thresholds met
- Cross-region or multi-system outage
- Executive or board-level visibility required

Communication Channels
----------------------
- Primary incident bridge and war-room channel
- Ticketing system for action assignments and status
- Executive summary distribution list for leadership updates
- Customer communications process coordinated through PR/Legal

Message Templates
-----------------
Initial update template:
- What happened
- What is affected
- What is being done now
- Immediate business guidance
- Time of next update

Status update template:
- Current containment state
- New evidence and impact changes
- Decisions made and owners
- Risks, blockers, and dependencies

Documentation Standards
-----------------------
- Timestamped timeline entries in UTC
- Decision log with approver names and rationale
- Action register with owner and due time
- Archive of all distributed stakeholder messages

Closure Communications
----------------------
- Final incident summary with impact statement
- Actions completed and residual risk statement
- Post-incident review schedule and ownership
